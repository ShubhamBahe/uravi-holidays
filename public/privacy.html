This policy explains how Uravi Holidays, Nagpur uses the information you share on this website.

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry we store the details you type in: name, phone number, email address (optional), travel date, number of travellers, budget and message. Our server also records your IP address to prevent spam.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, prepare quotes and manage your booking. We do not sell your information.

Who can see it

Enquiries are visible to authorised Uravi Holidays staff only. We may share the details needed to book services (for example a hotel or airline) with those suppliers.

Third-party services

Pages may load fonts, icons and maps from Google and cdnjs, and links lead to WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and review sites that have their own policies.
